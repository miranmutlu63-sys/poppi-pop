2. Data on your device

So that the game works, POPPI POP stores on your device: your progress (levels, stars, coins, collection), your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, color aid) and game statistics (for example play time, progress and whether you accepted offers). The game uses these statistics to tailor offers and ad breaks to you.

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or anyone else.

Device and country: The game asks once whether it may use your device (amount of memory) and your region setting to tailor offers. It only reads this information if you tap “YES” — and it also stays on your device. “NO” changes nothing about the game.

Deleting: You delete all of this data by deleting the app or by resetting your progress under Options in the game.

Legal basis (EU/EEA): Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (necessary for the game); for device and country Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG (your consent).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

In the game you can choose to watch ad videos to get a reward. The videos are delivered by Google AdMob: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Google processes, for example, your IP address, device and app information, information about the ads you watched and — only if you allow it — your device's advertising identifier. Purpose: delivering ads, measuring their performance and preventing fraud.

If you are 16 or older, a Google window asks you at the first start whether ads may be personalized (younger players only get child-appropriate ads, see section 9). If you say no, you only get non-personalized ads. You can block the advertising identifier at any time in the iOS settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can change your choice in the game under Options → ADS.

Google may transfer data to the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis (EU/EEA): personalized ads Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG (consent); non-personalized ads Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in offering the game for free). More: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. In-app purchases (Apple)

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland). Only Apple sees your payment details. The app only receives the confirmation of which pack was bought, so that it can credit it to you.

Legal basis (EU/EEA): Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performing the purchase). More: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Download from the App Store

When you download POPPI POP, Apple processes data such as your Apple Account and the time of the download under its own responsibility. We have no influence on this. Apple only gives us aggregated statistics (for example downloads per country) that do not identify you.

6. Game technology (Unity)

POPPI POP is built with the Unity game engine (Unity Software Inc., San Francisco, USA). Unity receives technical diagnostic data such as device model, operating system, performance values and crash information. It is used to find bugs and keep the game stable.

Legal basis (EU/EEA): Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a game without errors). More: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

When you open them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time to deliver the page and protect it from attacks; data may be transferred to the USA. We use no cookies and no tracking.

Legal basis (EU/EEA): Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. More: GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Contact by email

If you write to us, we only use your email address and message to reply to you. Afterwards we delete them unless we are legally required to keep them.

Legal basis (EU/EEA): Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

9. Children

POPPI POP is meant for all ages, including children. That is why the game asks for your age at the first start. The answer stays on your device. If you are younger than 16:

  • Google only shows child-appropriate, non-personalized ads — without advertising identifier and without tracking. The ad requests are marked as directed to children (as required, for example, by the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA).
  • The game does not ask about device and country and does not read this information.
  • The game shows no purchase offers on its own.

Parents can block in-app purchases on the iPhone under Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions or require their approval (“Ask to Buy” in Family Sharing).

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability (in the EU/EEA: Art. 15–18, 20 and 21 GDPR). You can withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Residents of California and other US states have similar rights; we do not sell or share your personal information. Just send us an email.

Since we receive no data from you through the app, we cannot give you information about your game progress ourselves — it is stored only on your device. For data held by Apple, Google and Unity, please contact them directly.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the authority in your country or state of residence.

11. Changes

When the game gets new features, we update this policy. The version published here always applies. If the English and German versions differ, the German version prevails.
