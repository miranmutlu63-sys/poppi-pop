Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. POPPI POP needs no account. If you delete the app or set up a new iPhone without restoring a backup, the game starts over.

How do I change the language?

In the game under Options → LANGUAGE. POPPI POP speaks 13 languages.

A purchase failed or did not arrive

Restart the game once while connected to the internet — a paid purchase will then be delivered. If it still doesn't work, write to us with the date of the purchase. You can request a refund directly from Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is inside the chests?

Below every chest you can buy, the game shows an ODDS button. There you can see exactly how likely each item is. For the Lucky Box, the odds are shown right below the stars.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ad videos are optional — you only watch them if you want a reward. Whether ads may be personalized can be changed in the game under Options → ADS.

For parents

At the first start the game asks for the player's age. Children under 16 only see child-appropriate, non-personalized ads and are not shown purchase offers. In-app purchases can be blocked on the iPhone under Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions or tied to “Ask to Buy” in Family Sharing.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app — this removes all game data, because it is stored only on your device. What Apple and Google process is described in the Privacy Policy.
